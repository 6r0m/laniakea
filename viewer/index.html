      Map guide

      Symbols

      Expandable groups have rings. Source files are boxes. External dependencies are diamonds. Dashed stars are outside the analysed target.

      An outer outline marks a project-listed target.

      Red stop signs and halos mark errors. Amber triangles and halos mark warnings.

      Connections

      Idle connections blend their endpoint colors. Trace colors show outgoing, incoming and within/self references.
